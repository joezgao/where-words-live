Where words live

Each dot is one of the 3,000 most common words in tiny-shakespeare. A small model, trained from scratch, placed them by guessing which words turn up near each other, so words used in the same company end up close. Pick a word to see its ten nearest neighbours. They are measured in all 48 dimensions; this 3D view is only a shadow, so some lines run long.
